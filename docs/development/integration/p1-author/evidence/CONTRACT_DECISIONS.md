Author Workspace applies `requireAuth`, `requireActiveAccount`, `requireRoles(['AUTHOR', 'ADMIN'])`, and `assertOwner`. | **ENFORCE_AUTHOR_WORKSPACE_SECURITY**: Deny anonymous (401), inactive account (403), foreign author (403). Pipeline requires serialized writer admission lease spanning Mongo CAS, PG metadata, snapshot, and accept. | **RESTORE_ADVISORY_WRITER_LEASE**: Port `chapter-writer.ts` with `withChapterWriter` lease (`pg_try_advisory_lock(hashtextextended($1, 71923))`).
