## New findings

| # | Severity | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| X1 | **Major** | Background analysis of a saved chapter (`incremental_story_analysis`, the outbox path) sends `extract_world_rules: false` and `extract_state_transitions: false`. So chapters written in the Author IDE never get narrative facts, world rules or state changes. Only events are extracted, unless the author clicks "Phân tích lại chương". Ch. 10 got 4 events and 2 aliases, but no facts or states. This explains why earlier chapters had facts or states only after a re-analysis. After #181 these steps cost only 2 calls (1 for facts and rules, 1 for states), so they could run on every chapter |
| X2 | Medium | Ch. 10's second event ("Thượng cổ Lôi Long truyền tải các đoạn ký ức đứt đoạn … vào thức hải của Trần Vũ") repeats ch. 9's first event in other words. It was not flagged, because the duplicate check uses quote containment and the quotes differ. The prompt's `already_recorded_events` did not stop the model |
| X3 | Medium | An author cannot drop a single proposal from a group. Rejecting the repeated event alone, or the re-proposed alias "làng Thạch Khê" (already approved), returned 422 "Reject the complete dependency group". The only choices were to accept all 6 or reject all 6. All 6 were accepted, so the repeated event is now in canon |
| X4 | Minor | Variant names decided automatically are proposed again as aliases each chapter, even when the alias is already approved ("làng Thạch Khê"). This is noise in the review queue |
| X5 | Minor | Ch. 10 does not say where Lam Yên is. In ch. 9 she was beside Trần Vũ when he entered the gate; in ch. 10 she appears only as a fading memory. The last sentence is narrator meta-text ("Chương mười khép lại trong tiếng sấm…") |
